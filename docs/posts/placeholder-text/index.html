Inspiration for a small LM project¶

https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.07759 TinyStories paper studied how large a model needs to be to be coherent and impressive in english language. We can think of usecases where the corpus is not huge, maybe words and text relevant to a specific task and train a small model of 5-10M parameters. It could be interesting to understand if we can Curate text-datasets via the best models out there that helps a small model become powerful as much as possible.

Next, the following paper asks about the probability and computations of rare outputs in LLMs. This can be studied in an interesting setting. https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.13211
